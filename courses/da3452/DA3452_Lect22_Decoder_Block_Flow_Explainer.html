Where tokens meet: one decoder block

Each coloured chip is one row of the N × d matrix, one token position. Its colours record which tokens its information came from. Watch when the colours mix, and when the lanes are sealed.

Attention mixes across rows

It is the only operation where row i reads from other rows, and the causal mask limits it to rows 1…i. Every colour blend in the animation happens here.

Everything else is row-wise

LayerNorm, the Q/K/V projections, the FFN and the residual adds act on each row alone with shared weights. That is why all N rows can go through as one matrix multiply.
