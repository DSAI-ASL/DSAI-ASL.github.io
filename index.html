Courses

Courses offered by the lab in the Data Science & AI programme. Lecture slides are published through the semester on the course page.

DA3452

Speech and Language Technology

Prof. Balaji Srinivasan  •  Jul – Nov 2026

Computational foundations of speech and language processing — text representation and Unicode, normalization, subword tokenization (BPE), and document vector representations such as Bag-of-Words and TF–IDF, building towards statistical and neural models of language.
